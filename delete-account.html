Privacy Policy

Delete your Fasmora account

You can request deletion of your Fasmora account and Fasmora-controlled cloud data associated with that account. Signing out is not the same as deleting your account.

How to request deletion

Send an account-deletion request from the email address associated with your Fasmora account. Include the words Fasmora Account Deletion in the subject and identify the account email you want deleted.

Email Fasmora Support Privacy contact

What may be deleted

  • Fasmora-controlled account records associated with the verified account.
  • Eligible cloud-synced browser metadata, such as bookmarks, reading-list entries, browsing history, open tabs, tab groups and selected settings stored by Fasmora Sync.
  • Registered device or installation records associated with the account where applicable.
  • Other Fasmora-controlled cloud data that is linked to the account and is eligible for deletion.

What may remain temporarily

Limited information may remain for a period where necessary to complete deletion across devices, preserve security, prevent abuse or fraud, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain limited backup integrity. Sync deletion markers may remain temporarily so deletions can propagate correctly.

What account deletion does not remove

Deleting a Fasmora account does not automatically erase browser information that remains locally on your devices. You can clear local browser data separately in Fasmora or Android. It also cannot delete information already provided to independent websites, search engines, Google, advertising providers, external apps or other third parties.

Verification

We may need to verify that you control the account before processing a deletion request. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, card details, recovery phrases or other secrets.
