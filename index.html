4. Browsing data and local storage

Fasmora stores browser information on your device so features such as history, bookmarks, downloads, reading list, offline pages, notes, tabs, permissions and session restoration can work. Websites can also store cookies, cache, local storage, IndexedDB, service-worker data and other browser-managed website data.

Local does not mean “never transmitted.” A locally stored item can be transmitted when you deliberately use a network feature that needs it, such as Sync, Send Tab or AI page context.

Normal browsing necessarily connects your device to websites. Those destinations can receive ordinary network information such as your IP address, request headers, cookies and information you submit to them. Fasmora does not control how an independent website uses that information.

5. Incognito browsing

Fasmora uses a separate Incognito browsing process and is designed to keep private-session activity separate from normal browser records. Incognito tabs are excluded from Fasmora account sync, cloud tab sync and Fasmora's category-only browser reliability telemetry.

Incognito does not make you anonymous to websites, network operators, internet providers, employers, schools or services you deliberately use. Downloads or files you intentionally save can remain on your device after an Incognito session ends.

6. Fasmora AI

When you use Fasmora AI, the app may send your prompt and relevant conversation context to Fasmora's AI backend and the configured AI service provider. Depending on the feature, this can also include a page title, URL, selected text, extracted page content, an image or a file that you choose to provide.

Sensitive-page protection

Fasmora includes client-side checks intended to reduce automatic page-context sharing from pages that appear sensitive, including sign-in, payment, banking, password, private-message and certain health-related contexts. These checks reduce risk but cannot identify every sensitive page or secret.

AI memory and conversations

AI conversation history and optional AI memory may be stored locally. Fasmora applies checks intended to keep obvious secrets such as passwords, one-time codes, API keys, card security codes, access tokens and recovery phrases out of persistent AI memory. Automated filtering cannot guarantee detection of every secret, so do not deliberately provide secrets to AI.

AI output

AI output may be inaccurate or incomplete and should not be treated as guaranteed professional, legal, financial or medical advice.

7. Voice Chat and transcription

Voice Chat requires microphone permission. When a voice session is active, microphone audio is transmitted for real-time AI processing. Fasmora obtains a short-lived session credential from its backend and may connect the voice session to Google's generative AI infrastructure. Audio input and generated output may be transcribed as part of the session.

Separate transcription features may send recorded audio to a Fasmora-operated backend or configured transcription provider. Fasmora does not require continuous microphone capture merely because the app is installed.

8. Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication and Sync

Fasmora offers optional account functionality. Google account selection may use Android Credential Manager and Google identity services, while Firebase Authentication is used as Fasmora's account authority. Authentication tokens prove your signed-in session.

If you enable Sync, Fasmora may process bookmarks, reading-list entries, browsing history, open tabs, tab groups, selected settings, timestamps, deletion markers, device identifiers and device display names. Send-to-device features also transmit the URL and title you choose to send.

The Sync design specifically excludes cookies, saved passwords or credentials, download files, local file paths, offline-page files, AI memory and Incognito tabs/history.

9. Safe browsing and URL reputation

For Fasmora's live reputation service, the app reduces a URL before sending it to the Fasmora security backend. The backend may receive the scheme, hostname, optional port and path. URL fragments and username/password user-information components are excluded by the client-side reduction. Results may be cached briefly on the device to reduce repeated lookups.

Other browser-engine, Android or website security mechanisms may independently contact their own providers as part of normal browsing and threat protection.

10. Advertising and consent

Fasmora integrates advertising technology that may include Google Mobile Ads/AdMob, Google's User Messaging Platform and Start.io. Advertising providers may process advertising identifiers, IP/network information, device/app information, ad impressions or interactions, fraud-prevention signals, consent status and approximate location inferred from network information.

Fasmora uses consent mechanisms where required and configures Start.io without assuming positive personalised-ad consent unless a compatible explicit consent signal is available. Where supported, privacy choices may be revisited from Fasmora's privacy options.

Fasmora does not sell users' personal information for money. Where applicable law gives a broader meaning to “sale,” “sharing” or targeted advertising, additional rights or opt-outs may apply.

11. Firebase, diagnostics, reliability and notifications

Fasmora uses selected Firebase services for functions such as authentication, messaging and diagnostics where enabled. Not every Firebase product is necessarily enabled in every release.

Fasmora's own browser-reliability telemetry is designed to be category-only. Examples include a page-failure category and whether compatibility or desktop recovery was used. That telemetry path is designed not to accept a URL, hostname, page title, search query, page text, origin or Incognito event.

Firebase Cloud Messaging may provide update, Send Tab, news or other supported notification payloads to the app. An FCM registration token may be assigned to an installation so messages can be routed to the device.

12. Device permissions

  • Camera: QR scanning and websites/features you authorise to use it.
  • Microphone: Voice Chat, voice input, transcription and websites/features you authorise for audio capture.
  • Location: websites or location features you explicitly permit.
  • Notifications: browser, website, download, Send Tab, news or update notifications where enabled.
  • Network/Wi-Fi state: connectivity awareness and browser operation.
  • Install packages: used when you explicitly choose to install a downloaded APK or Fasmora update and Android permits it.
  • Biometrics/device authentication: Android-provided protection for supported features. Fasmora does not receive your fingerprint or face template.

Website permissions are separate from Android app permissions. Granting Fasmora an Android permission does not automatically grant the same permission to every website.

13. Downloads and application updates

When you download a file, Fasmora may store download metadata such as source URL, filename, destination information, file size, MIME type and transfer status. Downloaded file contents normally remain on your device unless you choose to upload, share or open them with another service or app.

Fasmora may contact its update infrastructure to determine whether a newer version is available. Update requests may include the installed app version, CPU architecture, application identifier and ordinary technical/network information required for compatibility. Downloaded updates may be checked for integrity or signature information before Android handles installation.

14. Search engines, external apps and web apps

When address-bar text is treated as a search, the query is sent to your selected search engine, which may receive the query, IP address, cookies and browser request information under its own privacy policy.

When a website requests handoff to an installed app or supported web app, Fasmora may offer you a choice to stay in Fasmora or open the external app. If you choose the external app, information contained in the URL or intent may be passed to that app as required for the handoff.

15. Third-party services

Fasmora relies on or interacts with third-party technology including Mozilla GeckoView for web rendering, Google and Firebase services for supported authentication, messaging, advertising, consent and AI functionality, Supabase-hosted Fasmora backend functions, and Start.io advertising services.

Information processed by a service provider is not always information permanently stored by Fasmora itself. For example, an ad provider may process an advertising identifier, an AI provider may process a prompt to generate an answer, or a website may log your IP address without Fasmora storing a copy of that website's logs.

16. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, Fasmora may rely on performance of a requested service, consent, legitimate interests such as security and reliability, or compliance with legal obligations. The applicable basis depends on the feature and jurisdiction.

17. Data retention

Fasmora aims not to retain information longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was processed.

  • Local browser information: generally remains until you delete it, clear the relevant data category, an automatic expiry removes it, or app data is removed.
  • Incognito information: intended to be removed when the relevant private session closes, except files you deliberately save.
  • Sync information: may remain while needed to provide Sync. Server-acknowledged deletion markers may be retained for approximately 90 days so deletions can propagate correctly.
  • Account information: may remain while the account exists and for a limited period afterward where necessary for deletion, security, abuse prevention, disputes or legal obligations.
  • Security reputation data: may be cached briefly to avoid repeated identical lookups.
  • AI, voice and diagnostics: provider-side or temporary backend information may be retained according to Fasmora's configuration and the relevant provider's retention terms.

18. Account and data deletion

Signing out is not the same as deleting your account. Fasmora provides a public account-deletion page at delete-account.html.

A verified deletion request may result in deletion or de-identification of Fasmora-controlled account information, eligible cloud-synced browser metadata, device records and other associated Fasmora-controlled cloud data.

Some information may remain temporarily where required to propagate deletion across devices, preserve security, prevent abuse or fraud, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes or maintain limited backup integrity. Account deletion does not automatically remove browser information stored locally on your devices or information already provided to independent third parties.

19. How Fasmora protects information

Fasmora uses technical measures intended to reduce unnecessary exposure, including HTTPS connections, authenticated Sync requests, short-lived voice credentials, Incognito separation, restrictions on Sync data categories, sensitive-page checks before AI page-context sharing, reduced URL transmission for reputation checks, limited reliability telemetry and update integrity checks.

No software, device, server or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep Android and Fasmora updated and avoid sending passwords, recovery codes, payment credentials or other secrets through AI or untrusted websites.

20. Children and younger users

Fasmora is a general-purpose web browser and is not designed specifically as a service for young children. Websites and online services accessed through the browser may impose their own age, parental-consent and account requirements.

Fasmora does not intend to knowingly process children's personal information through account, AI, advertising or other network features in a manner prohibited by applicable law. Where special age or consent requirements apply, features may be limited or additional consent mechanisms may be required.
