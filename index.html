1. Scope

This policy applies to the Fasmora Android browser and Fasmora-operated online services used by the app. It does not control the privacy practices of websites you visit, apps you open from Fasmora, search engines you select, or independent third-party services.

“Fasmora,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of the Fasmora browser. “You” means a person using Fasmora.

3. Browsing data and local storage

Fasmora maintains browser information on your device so features such as history, bookmarks, downloads, reading list, offline pages, notes, tabs, site permissions and session restoration can work. Web content itself can also store cookies, cache and other website data through the browser engine.

Normal web browsing necessarily connects your device to the websites and services you request. Those destinations can receive ordinary network information such as your IP address, browser/network request information and data you submit to them. Fasmora does not control how an independent website uses information it receives.

Local does not mean “never transmitted.” A locally stored bookmark or history entry remains local unless you enable or invoke a network feature that uses it—for example account sync. Likewise, page content can be sent when you deliberately use an AI page-context feature.

4. Incognito browsing

Fasmora uses a separate incognito browsing process and is designed to keep private-session data separate from normal browser records. Incognito tabs are excluded from Fasmora account/cloud sync and from Fasmora's category-only browser reliability telemetry.

Incognito does not make you anonymous to websites, internet providers, networks, employers/schools managing a network, or external services you deliberately use. Network requests still have to reach their destinations. Features you explicitly invoke may also communicate with their providers where necessary to work.

5. Fasmora AI, page context, images and voice

AI chat

When you send a request to Fasmora AI, the app sends the prompt and relevant conversation history to Fasmora's AI backend. Depending on the feature you choose, a request may also include the current page title, page URL, extracted page context, and an image you attach or ask the AI to analyze. A per-installation client identifier may accompany AI requests.

Sensitive-page protection

Fasmora includes client-side checks intended to prevent automatic page-context sharing from pages that appear sensitive, including sign-in, payment, banking, password and certain private-message or health-related contexts. These checks reduce risk but cannot identify every sensitive page or every sensitive piece of information. Review what you send to AI.

AI memory and conversations

AI conversation data and AI memory can be stored locally to provide conversation history and personalization. Fasmora applies additional checks intended to keep obvious secrets such as passwords, one-time codes, API keys, card security codes and seed phrases out of persistent AI memory. Do not intentionally provide secrets to AI.

Voice

Voice Chat requires microphone permission. When active, audio is captured from your microphone and transmitted for real-time AI processing. Fasmora obtains a short-lived session credential from its backend and connects the voice session to Google's generative AI service. Audio input and generated output may be transcribed as part of that session. Microphone capture is started for the voice feature only after permission and when the voice session is used.

Separate transcription features may send recorded audio to Fasmora's transcription backend for conversion to text.

6. Google sign-in, Firebase authentication and Sync

Fasmora offers optional account functionality. Google account selection uses Android Credential Manager/Google identity services, and Firebase Authentication is used as Fasmora's account authority. Authentication tokens are used to prove your signed-in session; Fasmora's sync transport requests a current Firebase ID token rather than treating a public endpoint as authenticated sync.

If you use Fasmora Sync, the sync service can process portable browser metadata including bookmarks, reading-list entries, browsing history, open tabs, tab groups, selected settings, deletion markers, a randomly generated installation/device identifier and a display name for the device. Send-to-device features also transmit the URL and title you choose to send.

The current sync contract specifically excludes cookies, downloads, local file paths, offline-page files, AI memory and saved credentials/passwords. Incognito tabs are not eligible for sync.

7. Safe browsing and URL reputation

Fasmora includes browser security features designed to warn about unsafe destinations. For Fasmora's live reputation service, the app sends a privacy-reduced form of the URL to the Fasmora security backend: the scheme, hostname, optional port and path. URL fragments and user-information components are not included by this client-side reduction. Results may be cached briefly on the device to reduce repeated lookups.

Other browser-engine or website security mechanisms may independently contact their own service providers as part of normal web browsing and threat protection.

8. Advertising and consent

Fasmora integrates advertising technology including Google Mobile Ads/AdMob and Start.io. The Android app declares access to the Google advertising ID. Before requesting ads, Fasmora uses Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow to determine whether ads may be requested and to display privacy choices where required.

Advertising providers may process device identifiers, IP/network information, ad interactions, approximate location inferred from network information, device/app information and other data under their own policies and your consent/settings. Fasmora configures Start.io without a positive personalized-ad consent signal unless a compatible explicit choice is available. You may be able to revisit advertising privacy choices from Fasmora's privacy options where offered.

9. Analytics, reliability and notifications

Fasmora uses Firebase components. Its browser reliability telemetry is deliberately category-only: page-failure reporting records a failure category, and compatibility recovery records whether compatibility or desktop recovery was used. That telemetry path is designed not to accept a URL, page title, query, page text, host or origin, and incognito callers are excluded.

Firebase and related SDKs may process technical diagnostics according to their service configuration, including information needed for app stability, authentication and messaging. Firebase Cloud Messaging can deliver update or other notification payloads to the app. The app currently receives an FCM registration token; server registration may depend on the feature's implementation.

10. Device permissions

Fasmora declares permissions needed for browser and optional features. Android runtime permissions are requested when appropriate.

  • Camera: QR scanning and websites/features you authorize to use the camera.
  • Microphone: Voice Chat, voice input and websites/features you authorize for audio capture.
  • Location: websites or location features you explicitly permit.
  • Notifications: browser, website, download, news or update notifications where enabled.
  • Network/Wi-Fi state: connectivity awareness and reliable browser operation.
  • Install packages: used when you explicitly choose to install a downloaded APK or Fasmora update and Android permits it.
  • Biometrics: device-provided authentication for protected features where used; Fasmora does not receive your fingerprint or face template from Android.

Website permissions are separate from Android app permissions. A website cannot receive a permission merely because Fasmora itself has the Android permission; the relevant browser/site permission flow must also allow it.

11. Third-party services and websites

Fasmora relies on or can interact with third-party technology, including Mozilla GeckoView for web rendering, Google/Firebase services for authentication, messaging, analytics/diagnostics and advertising/consent, Google generative AI services for supported AI/voice functionality, Supabase-hosted Fasmora backend functions, and Start.io advertising services.

Search engines, websites, web apps, external apps and content providers you choose can have their own privacy practices. Their collection is governed by their policies, not this one. Fasmora may change service providers as features evolve; material changes to Fasmora's own handling will be reflected in this policy.

12. Data retention and deletion

Local browser records generally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the relevant browser data, remove the app, or an app feature automatically expires them. Some temporary caches have shorter lifetimes.

Cloud-synced information can remain on Fasmora's sync infrastructure as needed to provide sync and resolve changes across devices. Sync deletion markers may be retained for a period so deletions can propagate correctly; the current client is designed to prune server-acknowledged tombstones after approximately 90 days.

Third-party providers may retain information according to their own policies, legal obligations and service configuration. Removing local app data does not necessarily delete information already provided to a website or independent third-party service.

13. How we protect information

Fasmora uses technical measures intended to reduce unnecessary exposure, including HTTPS-only app network configuration, authenticated sync, short-lived voice credentials, separation of incognito browsing, sensitive-page checks before AI context sharing, restrictions on what sync may contain, and limited reliability telemetry.

No software, device or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and Fasmora updated, review website permissions, and avoid sending passwords, recovery codes, payment credentials or other secrets through AI or untrusted websites.

14. Children and younger users

Fasmora is a general-purpose browser and is not designed as a service directed specifically to young children. Websites and online services accessed through the browser may impose their own minimum-age requirements. Where consent or age requirements apply to an online feature, users should follow the rules applicable in their country and the requirements of the relevant provider.

15. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, or information about certain processing. Applicable rights and exceptions vary by jurisdiction.

Within Fasmora, you can also control privacy through practical choices such as clearing browser data, using Incognito, changing site permissions, disabling or avoiding optional sync, signing out, controlling Android permissions, choosing whether to provide page context or files to AI, and using available advertising privacy options.

If a request concerns information controlled by a website, search engine, Google, an advertising provider, or another independent service, you may need to contact that provider directly.

16. International processing

Fasmora's service providers may operate infrastructure in multiple countries. When you use network features, information may therefore be processed outside your country. Applicable contractual, technical or legal safeguards depend on the provider and jurisdiction.

17. Changes to this policy

Fasmora may update this Privacy Policy when the app, service providers, legal requirements or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published. Material changes should be communicated in an appropriate way where required.

18. Contact

Questions, privacy requests or concerns about Fasmora's handling of personal information can be submitted through Fasmora's official support/contact channel. A dedicated privacy contact address may be added here when formally designated.
